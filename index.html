    About me

    Motion & AI Designer

    I started in graphic design and grew into motion, building a profile that blends visual storytelling with a strong technical side. Today I combine design, animation and generative AI to create visuals for screens, streets and physical spaces.

    born in Valencia, now based in Barcelona

    I take projects from concept to final delivery — art direction, consistency and technical precision included. I’ve worked with marketing, content and dev teams on pieces for Real Betis, Valencia Basket, Hummel, Caprabo and TMM.

    Off the clock, I’m still designing — personal projects and new experiments. The rest of the time you’ll find me travelling, discovering new cultures and sharing good moments with friends.
